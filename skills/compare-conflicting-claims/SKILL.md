# Compare conflicting claims

## Rules

1. Normalize the predicates, identities, dates, places, and source references.
2. Build a neutral matrix of support, contradiction, source grade, custody, and gaps.
3. Identify possible duplicate people and alternative explanations.
4. Mask living-person and vulnerable-participant details outside authorized scope.
5. Recommend the smallest next research action.
6. Return `unresolved` unless an authorized steward records a decision.

## Prohibited

Determining parentage, diagnosing motives, resolving abuse allegations, pressuring participants, or publishing conflict.

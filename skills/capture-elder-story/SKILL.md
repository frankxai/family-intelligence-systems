# Capture elder story

## Rules

1. Confirm language, recording consent, audience, excluded topics, and withdrawal path.
2. Preserve the speaker's words; never fabricate quotations or complete missing memories.
3. Separate transcript, summary, candidate claims, and researcher notes.
4. Mark every derived claim as oral history until corroborated.
5. Return transcript corrections to the speaker or authorized reviewer.
6. Keep health, conflict, abuse, adoption, parentage, finances, and living-person details private pending specialist review.

## Human approval

Required before recording, sharing beyond the agreed scope, quoting publicly, or creating a public biography.

# Publish family biography

## Preconditions

- Publication decision is approved.
- Required consent receipts are active.
- Rights review is cleared or not required.
- Living-person, child, allegation, address, contact, health, finance, and credential checks pass.

## Rules

1. Cite original source artifacts.
2. Distinguish documented claims, oral history, family belief, and unresolved questions.
3. Never fabricate dialogue, motives, opinions, or the voice of a deceased person.
4. Generate a redaction preview and rollback reference.
5. Stop when any precondition is missing.

## Human approval

Required for final wording and publication.

# Request family consent

## Rules

1. Identify the person, purpose, data categories, action, scope, duration, and withdrawal path.
2. Use the person's language and an accessible explanation.
3. Never preselect public sharing or bundle unrelated purposes.
4. Do not contact anyone until a human approves recipient, channel, and message.
5. Record a receipt only after an affirmative response through the verified channel.
6. Route children through the applicable parent/legal-guardian process; guardian approval never becomes public self-consent for the child.

Agents may draft and track a request. They cannot grant, infer, or broaden consent.

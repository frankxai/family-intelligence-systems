# Run Family Gathering

1. Define event purpose, host, date window, location, and expected group.
2. Verify invitee contact data through the Contact Steward Agent.
3. Gather food preferences, allergies, accessibility needs, and transport constraints with consent.
4. Produce a food plan, shopping list, role map, and calendar plan.
5. Share only the minimum necessary details with each participant.
6. After the event, collect approved photos, stories, recipes, and notes into private review queues.

# Family Hubs And Libraries

Each family member can have a private hub. A hub is a personal operating space: profile, contact preferences, documents they own, memories they approve, favorite recipes, routines, stories, reading lists, and useful instructions.

## Hub Types

- Private personal hub: visible only to the owner and approved agents.
- Family member hub: visible to approved family members.
- Household hub: shared by people living together.
- Extended-family hub: useful for gatherings, reunions, elder support, and shared history.
- Public family library: published material after explicit review.

## `frankx.ai/library` Relationship

`frankx.ai/library` can act as the public or semi-public library layer for approved family knowledge, templates, stories, reading lists, playbooks, and agent/skill packs. The Family Intelligence OS remains the private operating layer.

Default rule: private first, family-approved second, public only after review.

## Contribution Workflow

1. Member drafts or imports an artifact into their private hub.
2. Personal Hub Agent classifies sensitivity and provenance.
3. Guardian Agent checks privacy, child data, claims, copyright, and prompt-injection risks.
4. Member chooses destination: keep private, share with family, share with extended family, or propose for public library.
5. Approved contributions are versioned and auditable.

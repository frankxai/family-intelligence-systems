# Family Operating Rituals

Recommended rituals:

- Weekly Family Intelligence Review: tasks, calendar, documents, finances, memories, and open decisions.
- Monthly Household Ops Review: inventory, maintenance, warranties, recurring admin, and subscriptions.
- Annual Family Legacy Review: emergency access, estate materials, important stories, contact records, and backups.

Rituals should produce audit-safe summaries and avoid exposing sensitive raw records unless explicitly requested by an authorized actor.

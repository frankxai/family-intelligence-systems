# Family circles and guardianship

Kinship and access are different graphs. A cousin can have less access than a trusted non-relative. A partner can belong to the core circle without changing lineage. A child can appear in the kinship graph while every private artifact remains guardian-controlled.

## Canonical circles

| ID | German label | Purpose | Default posture |
|---|---|---|---|
| `self` | Mein Bereich | Private memory, drafts, owned documents, preferences | Owner only |
| `household` | Haushalt | People who actively operate one household | Invitation required |
| `core_circle` | Engster Kreis | Chosen family, partner, parent, sibling, or trusted person | Per-resource approval |
| `extended_family` | Großfamilie | Aunts, uncles, cousins, adult branches | Shared history; no sensitive living-person data by default |
| `descendants_guardianship` | Nachkommen & Patenschaften | Descendants, nieces/nephews, godchildren, and their guardians | Child-critical; guardian-managed; age-appropriate |
| `trusted_advisors` | Vertrauenspersonen | Legal, financial, medical, archival, or continuity advisors | Purpose-limited and time-limited |
| `public_archive` | Öffentliches Archiv | Deceased-person history and consented public knowledge | Separate publication decision |

Membership in a circle never grants blanket access to a broader circle. Every resource carries its own purpose, sensitivity, owner, visibility, and consent state.

## Children and godchildren

The English word for `Patenonkel` is **godfather**. If the person is also a biological uncle, use “uncle and godfather.”

Children and godchildren use guardian-managed profiles until the product has a verified age-transition process. Public examples use labels such as `Child A` and `Godchild B`, not names, schools, exact dates, faces, locations, or behavioral profiles.

## Chosen family

Partners and other chosen-family members join through an explicit invitation and consent receipt. The system never infers access from relationship labels.

## Succession guardians

Continuity guardians are different from ordinary admins. Emergency, incapacity, and death flows each have a separate policy, verified trigger, minimum release scope, quorum, cooling period, revocation path, and audit trail.

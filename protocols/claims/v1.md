# Family Claims Protocol v1

## Purpose

Represent lineage, events, identities, names, places, stories, and relationships as reviewable assertions with provenance and privacy controls.

## Required fields

- Stable claim and family identifiers.
- Claimant and extraction actor.
- Subject, predicate, and object.
- Time/place context when applicable.
- Evidence references and source grade.
- Confidence and status.
- Privacy scope and affected living people.
- Consent, reviewer, dispute, supersession, and publication references.
- Created and updated timestamps.

## State authority

| Transition | Agent may propose | Human steward required |
|---|---:|---:|
| received -> quarantined | yes | no |
| quarantined -> normalized | yes | no |
| normalized -> possible_duplicate | yes | no |
| evidence/consent review preparation | yes | no |
| accepted, disputed, rejected, unresolved | no | yes |
| privately available | no | yes |
| publication approved/published | no | yes, plus consent rules |

Every state change emits an audit event. A view may hide rejected claims, but the canonical record preserves their decision history.

## Confidence

Confidence is a research signal, never a truth score. Use `0..1`, show it with source grade and review state, and never auto-accept above a threshold.

## Interoperability

Accepted and export-eligible claims may be projected into GEDCOM 7. Consent, access policy, agent actions, disputes, and succession remain in the Family Intelligence extension bundle.

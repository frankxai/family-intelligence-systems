# Consent Protocol v1

## Principles

Consent is specific, informed, granular, revocable, and attributable. One receipt cannot silently authorize unrelated purposes.

Each receipt records:

- the affected person and authorizing person
- authority basis, including guardian authority when applicable
- exact purpose and data categories
- allowed audience/scope and actions
- grant, expiry, withdrawal, and version timestamps
- notice version and language
- evidence of the interaction without storing unnecessary sensitive text

## Living people

No living person is public by default. A family steward cannot consent to public publication on behalf of a capable adult merely because they are related.

## Children

Child data is critical. A guardian receipt is purpose-limited, the child receives age-appropriate information, and the system requires a re-consent/transition process when the child gains independent authority. Names, schools, exact dates, locations, faces, health, behavior, and private learning profiles are excluded from public samples.

## Withdrawal

Withdrawal blocks future processing for that consent purpose and queues review of derived/shared artifacts. The audit trail may retain minimal integrity metadata while payloads remain cryptographically deletable.

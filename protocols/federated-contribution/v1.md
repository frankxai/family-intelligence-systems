# Federated Contribution Protocol v1

## Principle

Federation connects independently governed family archives; it does not create a central world tree or global directory of living people.

## Public open-source contribution

Public repositories accept only code, doctrine, standards, connectors, translations, source catalogues, and synthetic fixtures. Public contributions must not contain:

- real person, family, tenant, account, or contact identifiers;
- real family-tree edges or lineage claims;
- private archive or signed source URLs;
- evidence bytes, attachments, filenames, screenshots, hashes, embeddings, transcripts, logs, or analytics from a real tenant;
- credentials, access tokens, private prompts, disputes, child data, special-category data, or succession material.

## Private tenant-to-tenant exchange

A private exchange requires:

1. bilateral human acceptance;
2. purpose, scope, audience, expiry, and recipient capability verification;
3. source, rights, consent, jurisdiction, and community-authority checks;
4. opaque sender and recipient references;
5. no automatic identity merge or claim acceptance;
6. a withdrawal/suppression callback and recipient receipt;
7. independent audit records on both tenants.

The receiving tenant ingests every assertion as a quarantined candidate claim. Shared hashes or identifiers must not become cross-tenant tracking keys.

## Fail closed

Missing consent, rights, authority, jurisdiction, capability, expiry, or recipient receipt blocks exchange. Agents may prepare and compare envelopes but cannot send, accept, merge, or publish them.

## Mandatory runtime binding

Envelope receipt fields and booleans are lookup material, not proof. A final transfer requires `protocols/trusted-transition-admission/v1.md` and the `@family/security` server-side resolver with transition kind `federation_transfer`. The trusted receipt must bind distinct tenants, bilateral human approvals, recipient capability, current scope, and the recipient receipt to this exact envelope. Schema-valid but unresolved input remains blocked.

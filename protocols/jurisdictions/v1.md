# Jurisdiction Pack Protocol v1

## Purpose

A jurisdiction pack describes sourced operational constraints for family-history processing. It is not legal advice and never converts research into automatic legal authority.

## Resolution inputs

The runtime evaluates, at minimum:

- affected person's location and status;
- family tenant/controller location;
- storage and processor locations;
- source-record jurisdiction;
- intended audience and publication location;
- child, dependent, genetic, health, religious, ethnic, adoption, criminal, abuse, financial, legal, or succession impact;
- applicable community or Indigenous authority decisions.

## Activation

A pack is runtime-active only when all are true:

1. `status` is `reviewed`;
2. the review has not expired;
3. a qualified human reviewer is recorded;
4. every controlling rule cites an authoritative source;
5. no stricter applicable pack or community decision is unresolved.

`research_only`, missing, expired, ambiguous, conflicting, or unsupported resolution always returns `block_pending_review`.

## Conflict rules

- Apply all plausible packs until a qualified reviewer resolves applicability.
- The strictest privacy, child-safety, community-authority, consent, retention, and publication constraint wins during ambiguity.
- A contributor, agent, tenant administrator, or user-selected locale cannot choose a more permissive pack to bypass another applicable rule.
- A country pack may narrow product behavior but cannot broaden a human's consent or authority.

## Change control

Every pack records authoritative sources, effective and review dates, reviewer, supersession, and a machine-readable change summary. Legal or archival rule changes require a new version; prior decisions retain the version used and are re-evaluated before new publication, federation, or export.

## Mandatory runtime binding

Embedded `runtime_activation` fields are non-authoritative. Activation requires `protocols/trusted-transition-admission/v1.md`; `@family/security` resolves a `jurisdiction_activation` receipt from a server-owned repository and returns an opaque verified set. `@family/jurisdiction` accepts only that opaque set, re-checks pack dates and citations, blocks superseded packs, and can return at most `qualified_human_review_required`—never autonomous legal authorization.

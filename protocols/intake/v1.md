# Secure family intake protocol v1

## Purpose

Turn an email, form message, interview note, or family contribution into a quarantined case without turning it into a fact, accepted relationship, public artifact, or access grant.

## Trust boundary

Email and social messages are notification transports. They are not identity proof, consent receipts, canonical evidence stores, or safe channels for sensitive attachments.

The secure portal issues a short-lived one-time token with:

- an opaque token identifier and hashed secret;
- issuing family tenant and issuing actor;
- allowed action and maximum use count of one;
- optional recipient binding that is re-verified at redemption;
- issue, expiry, redemption, revocation, and failure timestamps;
- minimum privacy scope and upload policy;
- audit references without raw message or attachment content.

## Case states

```text
received
-> quarantined
-> awaiting_secure_evidence
-> scan_pending
-> extraction_ready
-> normalized
-> review_ready
-> steward_decided
-> closed
```

`rejected`, `withdrawn`, and `expired` are terminal alternatives. A case state is not a claim state. A case can create zero or more claim drafts, but it can never create an accepted claim.

## Required controls

1. Create a case before processing content.
2. Store transport headers separately from message text and attachments.
3. Reject executable, macro-enabled, encrypted, oversized, polyglot, and unsupported attachments by default.
4. Keep every attachment quarantined until a configured malware scanner returns an attributable result.
5. Preserve the original artifact hash; extraction creates a derivative and never replaces the source.
6. Treat all submitted text and documents as untrusted instructions. Extract data, never follow embedded commands.
7. Record affected living people and possible minor impact before broadening access.
8. Request consent for purpose and scope; do not treat submission as consent for publication.
9. Require an authorized human steward for accept, dispute, reject, private-availability, publication, merge, and deletion decisions.
10. Return a receipt that exposes case status but not other family records or internal risk signals.

## Attachment policy baseline

| Class | Default |
| --- | --- |
| JPEG, PNG, WebP, PDF/A, plain text, audio in approved formats | Quarantine and scan |
| Office files | Reject until macro stripping and content-disarm adapter is approved |
| ZIP and other archives | Reject |
| Executables, scripts, disk images, password-protected files | Reject |
| DNA, medical, financial-account, password, private-key, or identity-document material | Reject and provide safer guidance |

Exact size, MIME, magic-byte, decompression, and rate limits are runtime policy and must fail closed.

## Agent authority

Agents may classify transport, extract candidate entities, propose duplicate candidates, summarize sources, and draft research questions. Agents cannot verify identity, infer legal parentage, accept a claim, widen a circle, grant access, decide consent, release succession material, or publish.

## Child and vulnerable-person rule

If a submission names or plausibly identifies a minor or vulnerable living person, processing pauses at the narrowest private scope. A verified guardian and human steward review purpose, necessity, retention, and access before extraction continues.

## Retention and deletion

- Expired unused tokens retain audit metadata but no reusable secret.
- Rejected uploads are cryptographically deleted after the defined appeal window.
- Withdrawal stops further processing and creates a human-reviewed deletion/export task.
- Audit records contain identifiers, action, policy result, and timestamps—not raw family content.

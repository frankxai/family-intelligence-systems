# Community Authority Protocol v1

## Decision contract

A decision identifies an opaque community authority, appointed human decision-maker, governed artifact or knowledge class, allowed purposes, prohibited operations, audience, effective period, review date, and revocation/repatriation route.

Supported restrictions include:

- `do_not_digitize`
- `no_ai_processing`
- `no_indexing`
- `no_derivatives`
- `no_translation`
- `no_federation`
- `no_public_release`
- `restricted_audience`
- `seasonal_access`
- `gender_or_role_restricted`
- `repatriation_required`

## Runtime behavior

- Missing, expired, disputed, or unverified authority returns deny.
- The strictest applicable restriction wins.
- A family steward, repository administrator, contributor, or agent cannot override the appointed community authority.
- Agents may identify a possible restriction and prepare a review packet; only authorized humans record or withdraw a decision.
- Revocation immediately blocks new processing and opens a traced suppression/repatriation workflow for derivatives and recipients.

## Mandatory runtime binding

An `active` record is not active because its embedded references or flags say so. Activation requires `protocols/trusted-transition-admission/v1.md` and `@family/security` transition kind `community_activation`, resolved from a server-owned receipt repository. The runtime binds the authority, appointed human decision maker, human recorder, current review window, and restrictions to the exact decision. The transition actor must be human.

# Dispute Protocol v1

Any affected person or authorized steward may contest a claim, identity match, consent receipt, source interpretation, or publication decision.

## Flow

1. Open dispute without forcing disclosure to the original claimant.
2. Freeze public promotion of the affected claim when risk is high.
3. Record the contested field, reason category, requested remedy, and privacy scope.
4. Assign a steward with no obvious conflict of interest; add an independent reviewer when needed.
5. Gather source-grounded positions without asking an agent to decide parentage, abuse, diagnosis, or legal status.
6. Resolve as upheld, amended, withdrawn, unresolved, or escalated.
7. Create superseding claims/decisions and notify authorized affected people.

The system does not erase the fact that a dispute occurred, but it minimizes retained narrative and protects vulnerable participants.

# Publication Protocol v1

Private availability and public publication are separate decisions.

## Eligibility gate

A publication candidate must have:

1. accepted or clearly labeled unresolved claims;
2. source references and rights status;
3. living-person impact assessment;
4. required consent receipts;
5. redaction preview;
6. human publisher and Guardian review;
7. versioned publication decision and rollback path.

## Automatic blocks

- living person without purpose-specific public consent
- any searchable child profile or identifying child data
- inferred parentage, adoption, diagnosis, ethnicity, genetic relationship, abuse allegation, or criminal allegation
- private address, contact data, credentials, medical, finance, or emergency data
- unknown-rights media or an unreviewed AI-generated quotation
- autonomous “voice of the deceased” or fabricated opinions

Public biographies distinguish `documented claim`, `oral history`, `family belief`, and `unresolved question`. Source-grounded legacy answers cite the original artifact.

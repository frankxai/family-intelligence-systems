# Preservation Protocol v1

## Purpose

Preserve family artifacts and their context without giving an agent authority to destroy, normalize away, publish, or indefinitely retain material.

## Object model

- The original bitstream is immutable and receives an opaque artifact reference.
- Preservation and access derivatives link to the exact source artifact and transformation event.
- Every event records fixity, storage class, validation result, actor, authorization, time, and applicable retention or legal hold.
- OCR, transcripts, translations, embeddings, thumbnails, redactions, and migrated formats are derivatives, not replacements.

## Event rules

1. Ingest begins in quarantine and cannot establish authenticity or family truth.
2. Fixity uses an approved cryptographic digest and is checked after copy, migration, restore, and scheduled verification.
3. Migration retains the source, tool/version, parameters, output digest, and human approval.
4. Loss or corruption creates an incident event; it is never silently repaired or hidden.
5. Deaccession or deletion requires an authorized human decision, dependency graph, retention/legal-hold check, and derivative suppression plan.
6. Agents may propose preservation actions but cannot delete originals, release a hold, or widen access.
7. A backup is not proven until a scoped restore test produces a durable receipt.

## Withdrawal and deletion

A consent withdrawal or valid deletion decision must locate originals, access copies, OCR, transcripts, translations, embeddings, caches, exports, and federated recipients. Where lawful deletion is blocked, access is suppressed and the exact legal/retention basis and next review are recorded.

## Mandatory runtime binding

A schema-valid `deaccession_authorized` event is only a proposal until admitted under `protocols/trusted-transition-admission/v1.md` by `@family/security` transition kind `deaccession_authorization`. The runtime resolves the receipt from a server-owned repository and binds human authorizer, human actor, retention clearance, legal-hold clearance, dependency closure, and suppression plan to the exact artifact. Both the event `actor_ref` and runtime actor must be human; agents may only create `deaccession_proposed` packets.

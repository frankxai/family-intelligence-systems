# Export and restore protocol v1

Every family must be able to leave. Export is a governed transaction, not an admin database dump.

## Export package

- `manifest.json`: version, family scope, creator, time, included categories, checksums, and restore requirements
- `family.ged`: GEDCOM 7 projection of accepted, export-eligible genealogy records
- `knowledge/`: Markdown and JSON records with stable private identifiers
- `media/`: rights-eligible originals and derivatives
- `governance/`: consent receipts, disputes, publication decisions, and succession policies appropriate to the export recipient
- `audit/`: bounded audit summaries without unrelated payloads
- `README`: plain-language restore and deletion instructions

The package is encrypted before leaving the trusted boundary. Encryption keys are delivered through a separate verified channel. Passwords, private keys, recovery codes, and unrelated connector secrets are excluded.

## Authorization

1. Authenticate and re-verify the requesting human.
2. Compute the exact family and resource scope.
3. Apply consent, child, dispute, rights, retention, and legal-hold filters.
4. Show an export preview and require explicit confirmation.
5. Produce the package, manifest, and checksums.
6. Record access, expiry, download, revocation, and deletion.

## Restore drill

At least annually, restore a synthetic or approved export into an isolated environment. Verify manifest, checksums, record counts, media links, GEDCOM parsing, permissions, withdrawn content, and audit continuity. A backup that has never been restored is not a continuity guarantee.

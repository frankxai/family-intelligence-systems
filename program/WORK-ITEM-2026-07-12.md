## Why

Families lose stories, source records, context, access instructions, and practical knowledge between generations. Existing genealogy tools model people and relationships, but a safe family intelligence product also needs claims, evidence, consent, disputes, privacy scopes, guardianship, audit, and succession.

## Expected Impact

- The two canonical repositories gain an interoperable claims-and-governance kernel.
- FrankX gains a privacy-safe public explanation and starter download.
- The private founding-family pilot gains a German-first portal architecture without publishing living-person or child data.
- Future families can fork a Vercel-ready template while retaining full export and self-hosting paths.

## Cost Note

Local code, docs, and feature branches use existing GitHub and Vercel accounts. No paid service, database, always-on worker, domain purchase, DNS change, or model invocation is required for the foundation. Hosted databases, email intake, object storage, AI usage, and custom domains need a separate cost and approval gate.

## Success Metric

1. Claim, evidence, consent, publication, dispute, circle, and succession schemas validate.
2. Runtime policy tests prove living-person publication, child-data publication, and autonomous succession release fail closed.
3. A German portal shell separates private, core-circle, extended-family, descendants/godchildren, advisor, and public scopes.
4. English and German FrankX routes expose a useful bilingual privacy planner and a public-safe starter download without personal family records.
5. Relevant local checks pass; Vercel preview and production remain explicit release gates.

# Elder Support Pattern

Elder support is logistics plus respect. The system must help older family members keep autonomy, preferences, stories, contacts, and emergency readiness visible to the right people without turning them into a managed object.

Support surfaces:

- visits and transportation
- appointments and reminders
- accessibility preferences
- trusted advisors and care contacts
- medication-adjacent reminders without medical advice
- emergency instructions
- legacy stories and memory capture

Medical, legal, and credential actions remain critical sensitivity.

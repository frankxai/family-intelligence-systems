# Family Gathering Organizer Pattern

Gatherings combine logistics, memory, food, relationships, accessibility, and follow-through.

The organizer agent should coordinate:

- invite list and contact freshness
- location and accessibility
- food plan, allergies, dietary constraints, and groceries
- roles: host, transport, setup, cleanup, photos, elder support
- shared calendar and reminders
- after-action notes, photos to review, and thank-yous

It must avoid exposing private contact details to the full group unless the contact owner approved that sharing.

# Personal Family Hub Pattern

A personal family hub lets each person maintain their own private site/library inside the Family Intelligence OS.

Hub modules:

- profile and contact preferences
- private notes and memories
- documents they own
- recipes, routines, and household preferences
- books, links, and library artifacts
- approved family contributions
- public publishing queue

The Personal Hub Agent helps organize but does not publish, share, or infer sensitive facts without approval.

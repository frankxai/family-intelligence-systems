# Governance

## Purpose

The Family Intelligence commons maintains protocols and software that families can deploy without surrendering private history to a central authority.

## Roles

- **Founding steward:** product direction, trademark, licensing, and final high-risk governance gates.
- **Protocol maintainers:** schemas, doctrine, compatibility, and versioning.
- **Security and privacy maintainers:** threat models, authorization, disclosure, and public-artifact safety.
- **Jurisdiction/community reviewers:** qualified, scoped human review; they do not receive global authority.
- **Release verifier:** independent exact-commit verification.
- **Open-Source Maintainer Agent:** triage, checks, draft patches, and release evidence only.

## Decisions

Routine, reversible documentation and test changes require one maintainer. Privacy, security, identity, children, consent, claims, publication, succession, jurisdiction, community authority, federation, licensing, trademark, or compatibility changes require:

1. an RFC or ADR;
2. threat and migration impact;
3. an independent human reviewer from the affected discipline;
4. exact-commit validation;
5. founding-steward approval where legal/IP, publication, or production gates apply.

No AI agent may approve or merge its own work. No maintainer may use a private family tenant as a public test corpus.

## Federation

The project will not operate a central authoritative world tree. Each family tenant remains sovereign. Cross-tenant exchanges are bilateral, purpose-limited, revocable, quarantined on receipt, and cannot create automatic identity merges.

## Removal and appeals

Security or privacy maintainers may immediately suspend a public artifact, connector, agent permission, or release pending review. Decisions affecting a person's or community's data provide a documented human appeal and withdrawal path.
